    About

    Cogis searches the chat history you already have with AI assistants and gives you back pointers — titles, dates, and links into the original conversation. It runs in your browser, signed in as you.

    This page is a renderer. The search itself happens inside the Cogis Chrome extension: the page hands your query to the extension over a same-origin postMessage bridge and shows what comes back. ChatGPT is the only group wired up today.

    Install

    Cogis is not on the Chrome Web Store yet. Until it is, load it as an unpacked extension — the steps live with the source on GitHub.

    This site makes no network request of its own, writes nothing to your browser, and runs no analytics. Your query text never reaches the cogis.ai origin — it goes to the extension over the bridge and from there to the AI providers you are already signed in to.
